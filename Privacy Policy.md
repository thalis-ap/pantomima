**Last Updated:** 18 October 2026

## 1. Introduction

SymplyApps (“we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile application **Παντομίμα (Pantomima)** (“the App”) handles information when you use it.

Παντομίμα is a Greek-language party game designed for friends and families. It includes team-based gameplay, free play, timers, score tracking, sound effects, vibration feedback, adjustable settings, and a built-in collection of Greek game phrases.

**The App operates entirely offline and does not require an internet connection to provide its gameplay features. We do not collect, transmit, or share users’ personal information through the App.**

## 2. Information We Collect

The App does not require users to register, create an account, sign in, or provide personal information.

We do not collect personal information through the App, including:

- Names, email addresses, or telephone numbers.
- Account credentials or personal profiles.
- Precise or approximate location.
- Contacts, photographs, videos, or personal audio recordings.
- Payment information.
- Personal identifiers for advertising or tracking.

The App does not include online multiplayer functionality, user accounts, or a developer-operated online service for collecting gameplay information.

## 3. Local Data Storage

The App stores certain preferences locally on your device to provide its functionality.

These may include:

- Sound and vibration preferences.
- Appearance preferences, including light, dark, or system theme.
- Default game durations and other gameplay settings.
- Temporary game-session information, such as team names, scores, timers, and game state, while playing.

The App uses local device storage, including SharedPreferences, for persistent settings. Its Greek game phrases and audio effects are bundled with the App and do not need to be downloaded during normal gameplay.

This information is handled locally and is not transmitted to SymplyApps through the App.

You can remove locally stored App information by clearing the App's data through your device settings or uninstalling the App. The precise effect of uninstalling or clearing data depends on your device and operating system.

## 4. Internet Connectivity and Data Transmission

Παντομίμα is designed to operate 100% offline, without a runtime network dependency.

The App does not require an internet connection for gameplay, team management, scoring, timers, settings, or access to its built-in phrase collection.

The App does not send personal information, gameplay data, or usage information to SymplyApps or to a remote server.

Because the App is designed to function offline, it does not provide online accounts, cloud synchronization, remote game sessions, or online data backups.

This statement concerns the App's own functionality and does not cover independent processing performed by your operating system, app store, or device manufacturer.

## 5. Third-Party Services

The App does not intentionally integrate third-party services to collect personal information, serve personalized advertisements, perform user analytics, or track users across applications.

The App does not require third-party online services to operate its core gameplay features.

However, Apple, Google, and other providers may independently process certain information when you download, install, update, or use applications on their platforms. Such processing is governed by the relevant provider's privacy policy and settings and is outside the App's direct control.

## 6. Advertising and Payments

Παντομίμα is offered as a free game.

The App does not include advertising or advertising-tracking functionality as part of its described gameplay, and it does not collect payment information.

Any future introduction of advertising, analytics, in-app purchases, or other features involving personal data would require an appropriate review of our privacy practices and an update to this Privacy Policy where necessary.

## 7. Device Permissions

The App provides gameplay features such as sound effects, vibration feedback, timers, and screen appearance settings.

These features use the relevant capabilities of the device. The App does not need access to contacts, location, photographs, or personal files to provide its core gameplay.

The App's actual operating-system permissions are determined by its implementation and platform configuration.

## 8. Children's Privacy

Παντομίμα is designed as a family-friendly party game for general audiences.

The App does not require children or other users to provide personal information and does not provide online communication, public user profiles, or online multiplayer features.

Because the App may be used by children, we intend to maintain its privacy-preserving, offline design and avoid unnecessary collection of personal information.

The availability of an app to children may trigger additional legal or app-store requirements depending on the country, intended audience, and distribution settings. We will address any applicable requirements relevant to the App's distribution.

## 9. Data Security and Retention

Since the App does not collect or transmit personal information to SymplyApps, we do not maintain a developer-operated database of users' personal information through the App.

Gameplay preferences and other locally stored information remain on the user's device unless removed by the user or managed by the operating system.

We do not establish a separate retention period for personal information because the App is not designed to collect such information.

Users should be aware that the security, backup, and removal of information stored on their devices may also depend on their device settings and operating system.

## 10. International Privacy Rights

Because the App does not collect or transmit personal information through its own functionality, it does not maintain user profiles or a remote personal-data repository for users to access, correct, or delete.

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have privacy rights concerning information processed by other parties, including app stores or operating-system providers. You may need to contact those providers directly regarding their independent processing activities.
